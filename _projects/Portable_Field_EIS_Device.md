This project aims to translate the **ASTM D8370-22** standard for *Field Measurement of Electrochemical Impedance on Coatings and Linings* into a **portable diagnostic tool** for quick, on-site coating evaluation.


![Ongoing Work](/files/projects/images/EIS_port.jpg)



The device focuses on:
- **In-service QC testing** and **condition assessment**
- Measurement of **impedance at 0.1–0.01 Hz** — key for coating integrity
- **High sensitivity** to detect extremely small currents

The vision is to make **EIS-based coating health checks** accessible and practical for field engineers through a compact, easy-to-use instrument.
